How PoundDesk may earn money

When the live service launches, some links may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, PoundDesk may receive a small commission from the retailer or affiliate network. This should not increase the price you pay.

How links will be labelled

Commercial links will be identified clearly and prominently, for example with “Ad”, “Affiliate link” or an equivalent disclosure placed where users can see it before interacting.

Editorial independence

Commission availability will not by itself determine a deal’s score. Rankings are intended to use factors such as the claimed saving, unit price, loyalty requirements, confidence in the source, price history where available and offer conditions. Sponsored placements, if introduced, will be labelled separately.

Always check the retailer

Prices, stock and eligibility can change. The retailer’s page and checkout are the final source for price, availability and purchase terms.

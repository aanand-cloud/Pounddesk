OUR METHOD

How we will rank deals

Method preview · 13 September 2026

Current scores are illustrative. Production scores will begin only after live authorised data and validation checks are connected.

Proposed scoring factors

  • Saving against a credible reference price.
  • Unit price and like-for-like pack comparison.
  • Available recent price history.
  • Source freshness and confidence.
  • Loyalty-card, location, delivery and eligibility conditions.
  • Stock and expiry confidence.

What will not buy a better score

An available affiliate commission will not directly increase a deal score. Paid placements, if introduced, will be separated and clearly labelled.

Corrections

Retail prices change quickly. Users and retailers can report errors to deals@pounddesk.co.uk. Confirmed expired or incorrect offers should be removed or corrected promptly.

Who we are

PoundDesk is a UK deals-discovery project. For privacy enquiries, email privacy@pounddesk.co.uk. Full operator and postal details will be published before commercial launch.

The preview may process basic technical information required to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security logs. Vercel hosts the website on our behalf. Saved sample deals are stored locally in your browser and are not sent to PoundDesk.

Purposes and lawful bases

Technical data may be processed by hosting and security providers. Where information is processed outside the UK, appropriate safeguards should be used. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, security, dispute handling and legal obligations. Local saved-deal data remains in your browser until you clear it.
